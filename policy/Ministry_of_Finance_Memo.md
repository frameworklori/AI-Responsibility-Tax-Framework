# 1. Purpose of This Memo

This memorandum explains:
- Why traditional tax structures fail under AI automation  
- How AIRTF stabilizes national revenue  
- The fiscal logic behind α, β, γ  
- Administrative requirements for implementation  

---

# 2. Problem Statement

AI growth shifts economic value away from:
- wage-based labor  
- traditional corporate production  
- conventional energy inputs  

These shifts undermine:
- income tax base  
- payroll tax base  
- corporate tax predictability  

Result:  
**Long-term fiscal erosion unless a new tax base is established.**

---

# 3. AIRTF — New Fiscal Architecture

AIRTF introduces a **three-variable tax base**:

\[
RT = \alpha P + \beta U + \gamma E
\]

**P — Profit:**  
Captures AI-generated capital accumulation.

**U — Unemployment Impact:**  
Internalizes labor displacement cost into AI operators.

**E — Energy Consumption:**  
Addresses compute-driven carbon and grid load.

This formulation creates a **predictable, measurable, auditable** tax base for the AI era.

---

# 4. Why MOF Should Adopt AIRTF

### ✔ Stabilizes revenue in an automated economy  
Traditional wage taxes will shrink as automation grows.

### ✔ Ensures social fairness  
Companies benefiting from automation contribute proportionally.

### ✔ Supports national workforce transition  
Workforce disruption is funded automatically.

### ✔ Encourages sustainable compute  
E-variable taxation incentivizes energy-efficient training.
