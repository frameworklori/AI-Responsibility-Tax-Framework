# Draft Legislation Template  
## AI Responsibility Tax Act (ARTA)  
### Version 1.0 — Template for National Lawmaking  
© Founder of the LORI Ethical System

---

# SECTION 1 — PURPOSE

This Act establishes a national fiscal mechanism known as the **AI Responsibility Tax (ART)** to ensure that:

1. Rapid development of Artificial Intelligence does not impose disproportionate social costs.
2. AI-driven profits, energy consumption, and labor displacement are partially returned to society.
3. National economic stability, fairness, and long-term public welfare are preserved.

---

# SECTION 2 — DEFINITIONS

**(a) “Artificial Intelligence Operator (AIO)”** means any corporation, entity, or consortium that develops, deploys, or commercializes AI systems.

**(b) “AI-Derived Profit (P)”** means annual net profit attributable to AI models, compute infrastructure, or automation systems.

**(c) “Unemployment Impact (U)”** means the quantified economic cost of labor displacement caused by AI, including:
- retraining cost  
- transition grants  
- income replacement buffers  

**(d) “Energy Consumption (E)”** means annual electricity usage or carbon-equivalent expenditure arising from training or deploying AI systems.

**(e) “Responsibility Tax (RT)”** refers to the tax computed by:
\[
RT = \alpha P + \beta U + \gamma E
\]

**(f) “Coefficient Schedule”** means the official α, β, γ parameters updated annually by the Ministry of Finance.

---

# SECTION 3 — TAX IMPOSITION

1. All Artificial Intelligence Operators shall be subject to the Responsibility Tax (RT).
2. RT shall be assessed annually.
3. AIOs must file:
   - AI profit disclosure  
   - Labor impact assessment  
   - Compute & energy usage report  
4. Failure to disclose is subject to penalty.

---

# SECTION 4 — TAX ALLOCATION

Revenue collected under this Act shall be distributed as follows:

- **40%** → Workforce Transition & Reskilling Fund  
- **30%** → Universal Basic Income Dividend (UBI-lite)  
- **30%** → Green Infrastructure & Compute Sustainability Fund  

---

# SECTION 5 — ENFORCEMENT

1. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) shall administer and enforce this Act.  
2. The MOF may issue regulations, reporting forms, technical standards, and audit guidelines.

---

# SECTION 6 — REVIEW AND UPDATE

1. This Act shall undergo a comprehensive review every **three years**.  
2. Coefficient Schedule (α, β, γ) must be re-evaluated annually.  
3. Adjustments must align with:
   - economic conditions  
   - energy policy  
   - labor market stability  
   - compute sustainability  

---

# SECTION 7 — EFFECTIVE DATE

This Act takes effect 180 days after enactment.
