# Public Communication Guide  
## Explaining the AI Responsibility Tax to the General Public

### Audience:
- General citizens  
- Journalists  
- Legislators  
- Civil society organizations

---

# 1. Core Message for the Public

**“AI creates wealth, but also creates disruption.  
AI companies must return part of that wealth to society.”**

This is the fundamental idea of the AI Responsibility Tax.

---

# 2. How to Explain It Simply

### 🟦 The Problem  
AI companies earn massive profits.  
But AI automation replaces jobs and consumes enormous energy.

### 🟩 The Solution  
The Responsibility Tax asks AI companies to contribute back to society.

### 🟧 The Purpose  
- Help workers transition  
- Reduce inequality  
- Support green energy  
- Stabilize national budgets  

---

# 3. Talking Points for Media

- “AIRTF is not a punishment; it is a balancing tool.”  
- “If AI replaces workers, society deserves compensation.”  
- “The tax grows with AI’s power—fair and proportional.”  
- “This is the world’s first unified AI fiscal architecture.”  
- “AIRTF is a safety net for citizens in the automation era.”  

---

# 4. Public Q&A

### **Q1: Will AI companies leave the country?**  
No. AIRTF is proportional, transparent, and internationally alignable.  
Most AI companies benefit from stable compute policy and clear regulation.

---

### **Q2: Will this tax slow innovation?**  
No. Only irresponsible or energy-wasteful AI development is discouraged.

---

### **Q3: Is this another corporate tax?**  
No.  
It is a **new tax base created for the AI era**, not a simple extension of old taxes.

---

### **Q4: Who receives the tax revenue?**  
The public.  
Distribution:  
- 40% retraining fund  
- 30% UBI-lite dividend  
- 30% green infrastructure  

---

### **Q5: Why should AI companies pay more?**  
Because their systems create:
- profit concentration  
- job displacement  
- energy burden  

These must be internalized to maintain fairness.

---
