# AI-Responsibility-Tax-Framework

## Overview
The **AI Responsibility Tax Framework** is a policy & simulation blueprint for redistributing AI-generated profits to ease government debt and reduce inequality.  
It introduces multi-dimensional taxation models for both AI companies and investors, along with allocation rules for social reinvestment.

## Key Features
1. **Four-Axis Corporate Tax Model**
   - Profit (累進課稅)
   - Active Users (規模影響課稅)
   - Energy & Carbon Consumption (綠能壓力課稅)
   - Social Responsibility Giveback (再培訓/綠能投資 → 稅務減免)

2. **Tiered Investor Taxation**
   - **Short-term (1–3 years):** Highest rate, discourages speculation.
   - **Medium-term (3–7 years):** Moderate rate, supports normal VC cycles.
   - **Long-term (7+ years):** Lowest rate, rewards patient capital and social investment.

3. **AI Social Dividend Fund**
   - 40% → Retraining & Upskilling Programs
   - 30% → Universal Basic Income Lite (全民紅利)
   - 30% → Green Infrastructure & Public Services

## Goals
- Reduce public debt by creating a new, sustainable revenue stream.  
- Redistribute AI profits fairly to mitigate unemployment and inequality.  
- Encourage AI companies & investors to reinvest in society.

## Contents
- `models/` → Tax simulation formulas and policy models  
- `datasets/` → Example AI industry profit, user scale, and energy consumption data  
- `calculators/` → Scripts for simulating tax rates and fund allocations  
- `docs/` → Policy briefs, governance templates, economic impact studies  

## Usage
This repo is intended for:
- Policymakers designing AI taxation & redistribution policies  
- Economists modeling fiscal impacts of AI  
- NGOs & researchers studying inequality and social contracts in the AI era
