# The Five-Pillar New Fiscal Architecture  
### Compute · Capital · Robot · Sovereign · Public AI Works

---

## Overview

AI requires a new national fiscal model.  
Traditional revenue bases—income tax, corporate tax, consumption tax—shrink as automation rises.

The Five-Pillar System provides durable revenue sources for the AI era.

---

# Pillar 1 — Compute Tax (GPU × AI 算力稅)

### Formula:
\[
CT = \lambda \times \text{FLOPs} + \delta \times \text{EnergyCost}
\]

### Tax Base:
- Model training FLOPs  
- AI inference compute load  
- Data center carbon footprint  
- Frontier model training events  

### Purpose:
- Prevent compute monopolies  
- Internalize environmental cost  
- Recognize compute as a modern public resource  

---

# Pillar 2 — Capital Tax 3.0

### Three-tier system:
| Holding Duration | Tax Level | Purpose |
|------------------|-----------|---------|
| Short-term | High | Prevent speculation |
| Mid-term | Medium | Encourage steady growth |
| Long-term | Low | Promote co-building with society |

### Additional tools:
- Excess AI Profit Surtax  
- Anti-bubble stabilization measures  

---

# Pillar 3 — Robot / Automation Tax

Automation displaces labor and erodes salary-tax revenue.

### Tax triggers:
- Replacement of human labor  
- Deployment scale of automation  
- Software-based automation (AI agents, workflow engines)

### Usage:
- Workforce reskilling  
- UBI-lite support  
- Industrial transition grants  

---

# Pillar 4 — National AI Sovereign Share

The nation becomes a **co-owner of AI value creation**.

### Mechanisms:
- National LLM profit sharing  
- Frontier model licensing royalties  
- Sovereign compute pools  
- AI export and API revenue participation  

### Purpose:
- Convert AI growth into national dividends  
- Reduce inequality between private AI giants and the public  

---

# Pillar 5 — Public AI Works (AI 公共建設工程)

AI-enabled public infrastructure for the 21st century.

### Includes:
- AI education systems  
- National open-source models  
- Disaster prediction AI  
- Public dataset governance  
- SME digital transformation infrastructure  

### Funding:
- Responsibility Tax (RT)  
- Compute Tax  
- Robot Tax  
- Sovereign Share income  

---

# Combined Effect

The Five-Pillar Architecture ensures:

- Stable national revenue  
- Fair redistribution of AI-generated wealth  
- Prevention of extreme inequality  
- Sustainable compute and energy use  
- National resilience against automation shocks
