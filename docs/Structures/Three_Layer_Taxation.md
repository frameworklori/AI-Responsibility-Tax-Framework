# Three-Layer Responsibility Taxation Structure  
### Creator Layer · Investor Layer · AI Company Layer

---

## Overview
The AIRTF Responsibility Taxation Structure is built on **three distinct layers**, ensuring that responsibility is distributed proportionally across the actors who benefit from AI acceleration.

---

# 1. Creator Layer

Creators are divided into three groups:

### **1.1 Original Creators**
- Model architects  
- Algorithm designers  
- Foundational AI researchers  

**Responsible for:**  
Cultural direction, foundational technology, algorithmic stability.

**Eligibility for tax reduction:**  
Open-source contributions, education support, national projects, and green AI initiatives.

---

### **1.2 Application-Level Creators**
- Developers building AI-driven apps  
- Workflow designers  
- Domain-specific AI integrators  

**Responsible for:**  
Social impact of application-level deployment.

---

### **1.3 Derivative Operators**
- Enterprises scaling AI solutions  
- Industrial-level AI automation  
- Cross-country AI infrastructure users  

**Responsible for:**  
Large-scale societal effects (labor displacement, energy consumption).

---

# 2. Investor Layer

Investor taxation follows **Capital Tax 3.0**, reflecting their influence on AI ecosystem growth.

### Components:
- Short-term capital gains → **High rate**  
- Mid-term holdings → **Medium rate**  
- Long-term strategic investment → **Low rate**  

### Additional elements:
- Excess AI Profit Surtax  
- Anti-Bubble Buffer Tax  

**Purpose:**  
Prevent speculative bubbles and encourage stable, long-term AI development.

---

# 3. AI Company Layer

AI companies are taxed on **four structural axes**:

1. **AI Profit** — direct and indirect AI revenue  
2. **User Scale Impact** — size of economic reach  
3. **Unemployment Impact** — modeled displacement costs  
4. **Energy Consumption** — carbon, electricity, compute burden  

These variables reflect the real-world effects of AI deployment.

**Redistribution:**  
Funds flow to the AI Social Dividend Fund, supporting labor transition, UBI-lite, and public AI infrastructure.

---

| Investor | Capital holders, funds | Stabilizing investment, preventing bubbles |
