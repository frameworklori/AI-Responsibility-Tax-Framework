# Falsifiability Layer

All thresholds below are provisional and require calibration.

## Compute Concentration Indicator

```text
Warning: Top 5 providers control >60% of frontier compute access
Crisis: Top 5 providers control >80%
```

## Labor Income Share Indicator

```text
Warning: labor share declines for 3 consecutive years
Crisis: labor share falls below calibrated historical baseline
```

## Demand Fragility Indicator

```text
Warning: productivity growth exceeds median income growth for 3-5 years
Crisis: productivity rises while median real purchasing power declines
```

## Money Velocity Indicator

```text
Warning: M2 velocity declines for 3 consecutive years while asset concentration rises
Crisis: money velocity remains below long-term baseline while household debt rises
```

## White-Collar Scarring Indicator

```text
Warning: underemployment rises among college-educated workers
Crisis: home ownership, fertility, and durable-goods purchases fall among prime-age white-collar households
```

## Platform Rent Indicator

```text
Warning: platform fee growth exceeds GDP growth
Crisis: platform rent extraction exceeds labor income growth in affected sectors
```
