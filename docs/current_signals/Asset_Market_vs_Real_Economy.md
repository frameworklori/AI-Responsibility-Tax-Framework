# Asset Market vs. Real Economy

AI stocks, major indexes, and large-cap technology valuations may remain strong even while consumer cash flow weakens. AIRTF treats this divergence as a critical current signal because asset strength can hide demand fragility.

AI does not automatically destroy the economy. The danger is that productivity gains may concentrate faster than purchasing power can circulate.

## Capitalization Is Not Circulation

Stock-market capitalization measures the market value of ownership claims. Wage circulation measures income moving through households into everyday consumption. These are related, but they are not the same system.

A rising market can reflect profit margins, AI infrastructure expectations, index concentration, buybacks, global capital flows, and the wealth effect among asset holders. It does not prove that middle-class and working-class households have stronger monthly cash flow.

## Concentrated Wealth Effect

The wealth effect is concentrated among households that own meaningful financial assets. Those households may spend somewhat more when markets rise, but high-wealth households do not scale everyday consumption proportionally with portfolio gains.

By contrast, wages paid to broad households usually circulate quickly through rent, food, transport, healthcare, retail, services, education, and local business revenue.

## AI Infrastructure Valuation Cycle

AI infrastructure can create a valuation cycle where chip suppliers, cloud platforms, data-center operators, model companies, enterprise software firms, and energy-linked infrastructure attract capital because future productivity gains are expected to be large.

That cycle can raise corporate profit expectations while household liquidity weakens if the gains accrue mainly to owners and platforms rather than wages and distributed purchasing power.

## Policy Misreading Risk

Governments may misread asset strength as economic strength. If policymakers treat rising indexes as proof of broad health, they may miss consumer debt stress, weak real wages, slowing money velocity, white-collar layoffs, and local demand fragility.

This document relates to [Platform_Rent_Ratio.md](../indicators/Platform_Rent_Ratio.md) and [Compute_Concentration_Index.md](../indicators/Compute_Concentration_Index.md), because asset-market gains can be amplified when platform rents and compute ownership concentrate.

## AIRTF Interpretation

The core risk is divergence between corporate profit and household liquidity. AIRTF does not oppose productive AI growth. It asks whether the value created by AI circulates widely enough to preserve civilization liquidity.
