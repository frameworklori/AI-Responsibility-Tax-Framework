# Post-Wage Civilization Risk

A post-wage economy may preserve survival through subsidies, AI dividends, social transfers, or digital public services.

That would be a major achievement. But income replacement alone does not replace participation.

Work and production participation also provide:

- skill formation
- identity
- social relationships
- causal agency
- negotiation over what society values

UBI or AI-managed provisioning may replace income, but not necessarily participation.

Economic agency is not only the ability to consume; it is the ability to participate in how value is created and distributed.

If people receive purchasing power without meaningful participation, society may avoid absolute poverty while still weakening human agency. Citizens may become consumers of AI-mediated abundance rather than co-authors of economic direction.

AIRTF therefore treats post-wage stability as a two-layer problem:

- liquidity: can people survive and consume
- agency: can people shape demand, access, production, and value distribution

Tax policy and AI dividends may help stabilize the first layer. They do not automatically solve the second.
