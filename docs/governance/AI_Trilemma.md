# AI Trilemma

Society may not be able to maximize all three at once:

1. rapid frontier AI development
2. distributed infrastructure ownership
3. public-sector leadership

Frontier AI currently requires massive compute concentration, large capital expenditure, technical talent concentration, and vertically integrated infrastructure.

## Sequencing Principle

```text
development-stage concentration may be tolerated temporarily
but deployment-stage dependency must be prevented
```

The goal is to prevent concentrated development from becoming permanent civilization dependency.
