# Actor Incentive Map

## Reform Supporters

Reform supporters benefit from fair infrastructure access, broad demand stability, public optionality, and lower platform dependency.

## Reform Blockers

Likely blockers benefit from concentration, rent extraction, vertical integration, and control over compute or distribution chokepoints.

## Coalition Logic

The strongest coalition combines middle-income households, small businesses, startups, public institutions, pension funds, insurance firms, and governments that fear platform dependency.
