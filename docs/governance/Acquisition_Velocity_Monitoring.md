# Acquisition Velocity Monitoring

Acquisition Velocity Monitoring tracks how quickly dominant firms absorb startups, talent, products, datasets, and APIs.

## Why It Matters

Large AI companies can absorb future competition before it matures.

## Indicators

- acquisitions per year
- acqui-hire concentration
- API absorption
- exclusive partnership velocity
- startup dependency on dominant cloud/model providers
- post-acquisition shutdown or integration rate

High acquisition velocity can indicate integration-layer monopoly risk.
