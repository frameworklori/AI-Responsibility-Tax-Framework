# Taiwan Case Study

Taiwan is strategically relevant because of its semiconductor ecosystem, export dependence, geopolitical risk, and advanced digital public capacity.

Potential research questions:

- How does chip centrality affect AI-era sovereignty?
- What public compute strategy is realistic for a small advanced economy?
- How can Taiwan reduce dependency on foreign model/platform layers?
- Which transition policies protect workers in export-facing sectors?

This is a research template, not an official policy recommendation.
