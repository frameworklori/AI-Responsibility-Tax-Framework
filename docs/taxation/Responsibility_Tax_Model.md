# Responsibility Tax Model

Original formula:

```text
RT = alpha * Profit
   + beta * UnemploymentImpact
   + gamma * EnergyConsumption
```

## Caveat

UnemploymentImpact is difficult to attribute directly. Worker displacement may come from AI, macroeconomic shifts, outsourcing, firm restructuring, or product demand changes.

## AI-Linked Displacement Score

To reduce attribution weakness, AIRTF adds an AI-Linked Displacement Score using:

- AI adoption rate
- workforce compression speed
- revenue per employee growth
- human-to-AI replacement ratio
- AI-generated output ratio
- department-level automation evidence

The score does not prove causation. It estimates AI linkage strength for policy review.
