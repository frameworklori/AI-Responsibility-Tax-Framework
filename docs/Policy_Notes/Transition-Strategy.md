# Workforce Transition & Industrial Shock Strategy  
### AI-Responsibility-Tax Framework (AIRTF)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Audience:** Ministries of Labor, Industry, Education, Economic Affairs

---

# 1. Purpose

To provide a structured strategy for transitioning national labor markets and industries affected by:

- AI automation  
- Large-scale job displacement  
- Shifts from human labor to machine productivity  
- High-skill vs low-skill labor divergence  

AIRTF funds (RT + Robot Tax + Sovereign Share) support these transitions.

---

# 2. Workforce Transition Strategy

## **2.1 Reskilling Pathways**
Three tiers:

1. **Tier A: High-impact transition programs**  
   AI engineering, data analysis, robot operations.

2. **Tier B: Medium-skill evolution**  
   Logistics systems, predictive maintenance, digital administration.

3. **Tier C: Rapid-shift training**  
   Elder care, service roles, creative industries.

---

## **2.2 Guaranteed Transition Funding**
Using:
- 40% of Responsibility Tax  
- 30% of Robot Tax  
- Sovereign Share profits  

Funds cover:
- Tuition  
- Career counseling  
- Certification  
- Paid training leave  

---

## **2.3 Employment Shock Absorption Mechanism**
Triggered when:
- Sector unemployment rises > X%  
- AI adoption exceeds threshold  

Government deploys:
- Wage subsidies  
- Sectoral transition grants  
- Temporary automation slowdown measures  

---

# 3. Industrial Transformation Strategy

## **3.1 Sectoral Vulnerability Index (SVI)**
Ranks industries by:

- Automation rate  
- Skill redundancy  
- Economic centrality  
- Worker replacement difficulty  

## **3.2 Targeted Industrial Stimulus**
For:
- SMEs needing digital support  
- Regions hit hardest by automation  
- High-energy-consumption AI sectors  

---

# 4. Education Strategy

## **4.1 AI-Era Curriculum Reform**
Includes:
- Logic literacy  
- Systems thinking  
- Cross-disciplinary fluency  
- Human-AI coordination  

## **4.2 National Talent Redistribution Model**
Aligns with:
- Sovereign Compute Pool  
- Public AI Works programs  
- National LLM deployment  

---

# 5. Outcome Expectations

- Reduced unemployment shock  
- Increased national adaptation speed  
- Equitable access to AI-era opportunities
