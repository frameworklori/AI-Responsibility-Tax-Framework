# Inflation and Cost Transmission Risk

AI infrastructure ownership participation only matters if real purchasing power improves rather than being offset by rising living costs.

AI infrastructure dividends may fail to improve real household stability if infrastructure concentration simultaneously increases:

- electricity costs
- housing costs
- insurance costs
- healthcare costs
- essential utility pricing

## Current Reality Signal

- AI infrastructure buildout is increasing pressure on electricity grids and utility investment.
- Utility spending and rate-hike requests are increasing in response to data-center expansion.
- AI infrastructure demand may create inflationary pressure in power, cooling, copper, chips, and construction.
- AI infrastructure investment may benefit asset owners while raising household costs.

## AIRTF Structural Mapping

- Civilization_Liquidity_Risk: higher essential costs can weaken real household circulation even if nominal income or dividends rise.
- Asset-owner concentration: infrastructure returns may accrue to capital holders before cost relief reaches households.
- Infrastructure rent extraction: utilities, compute providers, and real-estate-linked infrastructure can become rent channels.
- Demand fragility: households facing higher fixed costs may reduce discretionary consumption.
- Household purchasing power erosion: nominal AI dividends can be neutralized by electricity, housing, insurance, healthcare, or utility inflation.

## Possible Development Path

- A. Productivity gains reduce household costs.
- B. Infrastructure rent inflation offsets ownership gains.
- C. AI utilities become quasi-monopoly pricing systems.
- D. AI productivity and living-cost reductions partially balance.

## Indicators to Watch

- electricity inflation
- utility rate increases
- housing inflation
- insurance inflation
- healthcare burden
- AI service pricing
- real disposable income

## What Would Falsify This Risk

- AI lowers essential living costs broadly
- real purchasing power improves sustainably
- infrastructure productivity gains outweigh cost inflation
- utility pricing remains competitive and regulated

## Open Questions

Can AI-era infrastructure ownership improve real living standards if infrastructure costs themselves continue rising?
