# AI-IOPM Feasibility Assessment

AI-IOPM is an exploratory policy module for evaluating whether AI infrastructure ownership participation can support broader economic participation without replacing markets, wages, competition, or existing AIRTF tax architecture.

## Feasibility Question

Can AI-era infrastructure productivity be linked to broad participation in a way that improves real household stability, preserves investment incentives, and avoids symbolic ownership without governance power?

## Real-World Constraints

- infrastructure inflation
- utility pricing pressure
- hardware obsolescence
- debt-financed hyperscaler expansion
- global competition
- concentrated initial ownership
- governance reconcentration risk

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does participation improve real disposable income after living-cost changes?
- Does ownership access reach younger and lower-wealth households?
- Does governance remain distributed rather than concentrated through intermediaries?
- Does the system preserve investment incentives and global competitiveness?
- Does the model remain resilient to infrastructure depreciation and technology shifts?
