# Political Economic Management Requirements

AI-IOPM requires institutional management to prevent participation from becoming symbolic, concentrated, or too slow to matter.

## Core Management Requirements

- clear legal status for participation claims
- transparent valuation and reporting
- safeguards against capture
- compatibility with market competition
- coordination with existing AIRTF tax and public infrastructure tools
- measurable household impact

## Anti-Reconcentration Requirements

- prevent excessive asset-manager voting concentration
- require transparent proxy governance
- protect citizen governance rights
- prevent symbolic ownership structures

## Generational Participation Requirements

- youth participation access
- citizen starter allocation mechanisms
- intergenerational ownership balancing
