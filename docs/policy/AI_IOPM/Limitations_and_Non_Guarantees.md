# Limitations and Non-Guarantees

AI-IOPM is a participation framework, not a guarantee of permanent prosperity or equality.

AI-IOPM should be viewed as a possible stabilization and participation mechanism, not a utopian solution.

It:

- does not eliminate inequality
- does not guarantee fixed income
- does not replace wages
- does not prevent all concentration
- does not prevent speculative cycles
- does not guarantee political stability
- may fail under rapid disruption scenarios
- may require ongoing institutional redesign

AI-IOPM is intended to reduce the probability that AI-era infrastructure productivity becomes permanently disconnected from broad human participation. It is not designed to eliminate markets or remove the need for wages, entrepreneurship, competition, regulation, fiscal policy, or democratic governance.
