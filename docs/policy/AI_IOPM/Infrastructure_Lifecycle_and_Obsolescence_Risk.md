# Infrastructure Lifecycle and Obsolescence Risk

AI infrastructure participation systems must account for hardware depreciation, technology cycles, energy transitions, and oversupply risk.

## Current Reality Signal

- AI infrastructure spending is historically unprecedented.
- Data-center hardware depreciates rapidly.
- Compute architectures evolve quickly.
- Energy and chip bottlenecks may reverse cyclically.

## AIRTF Structural Mapping

- Infrastructure volatility: AI infrastructure may behave less like stable public utility capital and more like cyclical technology capital.
- Speculative cycle risk: high expectations can drive overbuilding, leverage, and correction.
- Debt-financed AI expansion: infrastructure returns may be vulnerable if utilization, pricing, or monetization disappoint.

## Possible Development Path

- A. Sustainable infrastructure demand.
- B. Oversupply cycle.
- C. Stranded data-center assets.
- D. Obsolete semiconductor facilities.
- E. AI capex bubble correction.

## Indicators to Watch

- data-center vacancy
- hardware depreciation
- compute utilization
- AI infrastructure debt levels
- power-demand mismatch
- semiconductor pricing cycles

## What Would Falsify This Risk

- infrastructure utilization remains structurally high
- AI monetization supports capex
- technology refresh cycles remain economically sustainable

## Open Questions

Can AI infrastructure maintain utility-like stability despite rapid technological obsolescence?
