# Transition Timing and Deployment Risk

The viability of AI-IOPM depends heavily on whether institutions can form faster than labor displacement and ownership concentration accelerate.

## Current Reality Signal

- AI adoption may move faster than governance adaptation.
- Labor-market disruption may occur before participation systems mature.
- Infrastructure ownership systems require legal and financial buildout time.

## Possible Development Path

- A. Gradual transition allows institutional adaptation.
- B. Rapid displacement overwhelms adaptation capacity.
- C. Hybrid transition with partial stabilization.
- D. Delayed implementation increases inequality lock-in.

## Indicators to Watch

- labor-displacement speed
- AI adoption speed
- legal implementation timelines
- infrastructure participation growth
- household liquidity deterioration

## What Would Falsify This Risk

- institutions scale rapidly
- labor adaptation keeps pace
- ownership participation expands before major displacement

## Open Questions

Can institutional redesign occur fast enough to prevent AI-era ownership lock-in?
