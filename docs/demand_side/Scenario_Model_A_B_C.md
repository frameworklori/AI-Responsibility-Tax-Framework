# Three AI-Era Scenarios

## Scenario A: Managed Transition

Characteristics:

- redistribution begins early
- public AI infrastructure is built
- sovereign AI equity exists
- compute concentration is monitored
- open-source ecosystems remain viable
- middle-class purchasing power is preserved
- labor transition infrastructure is funded

Outcome: AI growth remains socially absorbed.

## Scenario B: Credit-Extended Stagnation

Characteristics:

- AI productivity rises
- markets remain elevated
- GDP remains positive
- middle-class purchasing power stagnates
- consumption is maintained by credit and transfers
- social fragmentation slowly rises

Outcome: the system avoids immediate collapse but becomes increasingly hollow.

This may be the most likely default path without structural reform.

## Scenario C: Circulation Collapse

Characteristics:

- white-collar compression accelerates
- household debt reaches limits
- credit expansion slows
- public transfers become politically constrained
- consumption weakens
- service-sector employment falls
- fiscal pressure rises
- political instability grows

Outcome: productivity remains high, but circulation fails.
