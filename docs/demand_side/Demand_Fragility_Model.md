# Demand Fragility Model

Demand fragility rises when AI productivity grows faster than broad purchasing power.

## Mechanism

```text
AI threatens white-collar job stability
-> middle-class households reduce discretionary spending
-> home purchases and durable goods purchases are delayed
-> banks tighten credit standards
-> consumer credit slows
-> retail and service-sector demand weakens
-> secondary layoffs occur
-> initial fear becomes actual economic contraction
```

This self-reinforcing loop can begin with fear, uncertainty, reduced confidence, delayed purchasing, and credit tightening. It does not require immediate mass unemployment.
