# Marginal Propensity to Consume and Money Velocity

Lower-income and middle-income households tend to spend a much higher share of each additional dollar than the ultra-wealthy.

This means:

```text
distribution determines circulation speed
```

A redistribution model in the AI era should not only ask, "How do we reduce inequality?"

It should ask:

```text
How do we preserve economic circulation?
```

Money paid to broad consumer groups tends to circulate through rent, food, transportation, education, healthcare, retail, and services. Money concentrated at the top is more likely to flow into assets, equity accumulation, infrastructure ownership, political influence, and long-term capital storage.
