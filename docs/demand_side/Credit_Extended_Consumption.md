# Credit-Extended Consumption

Modern economies often maintain consumption through:

- household debt
- credit cards
- mortgages
- student loans
- auto loans
- buy-now-pay-later systems
- low interest rates
- public transfers

Credit can hide weak purchasing power, but credit is not permanent demand. It is borrowed future demand.

If AI weakens labor income while consumption is maintained by debt, the system becomes fragile.

Historical analogy: 1920s consumer credit expansion before the Great Depression.
