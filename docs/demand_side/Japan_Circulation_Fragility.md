# Japan Circulation Fragility

Japan is a secondary case study and warning about long-term circulation weakness.

Japan's post-1990 stagnation shows:

- money supply does not guarantee circulation
- low interest rates do not guarantee demand recovery
- asset collapse can cause long-term confidence damage
- demographic weakness reinforces low demand
- chronic stagnation may be more likely than sudden collapse

Japan demonstrates that an economy can remain technically functional while losing circulation momentum.

This matters because the AI era may produce long-term low-demand stagnation rather than sudden collapse.
