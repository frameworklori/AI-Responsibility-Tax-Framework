## 1. Purpose of AIRTF

The AI-Responsibility-Tax Framework (AIRTF) defines a fiscal architecture that aligns the rapid acceleration of AI with proportional social responsibility. Its primary goals are:

- To rebalance national taxation in a world where automation outpaces salary-based tax revenue  
- To prevent excessive concentration of AI-generated wealth  
- To ensure transparent, auditable redistribution of AI gains to society  
- To provide policymakers with quantitative tools that measure AI’s economic and labor impact  

AIRTF is designed as a modular, adaptable system capable of integrating into national fiscal policies without locking into any political ideology.

---

## 2. Core Components of the Framework

AIRTF consists of four fundamental layers:

1. **Three-Layer Responsibility Taxation Structure**  
   - Creator Layer  
   - Investor Layer  
   - AI Company Layer  

2. **Responsibility Tax Formula**  
   A mathematical model linking AI profit, unemployment impact, and energy consumption.

3. **Five-Pillar New Fiscal Architecture**  
   - Compute Tax  
   - Capital Tax 3.0  
   - Robot / Automation Tax  
   - National AI Sovereign Share  
   - Public AI Works Model  

4. **Redistribution Mechanism (AI Social Dividend Fund)**  
   Ensures that society receives direct, measurable benefits.

---

## 3. Guiding Principles

AIRTF is built on three ethical and economic principles:

### **Principle 1: Acceleration = Responsibility**
AI’s exponential growth must be matched by equally strong accountability measures.

### **Principle 2: Wealth Circulation, Not Concentration**
AI must not become a force that channels resources solely to major corporations and investors.

### **Principle 3: Sustainability and Social Stability**
A well-designed AI fiscal system reduces social anxiety and prevents labor shocks.
