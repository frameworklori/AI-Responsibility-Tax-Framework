# Social Stability & Transition Layer

## Purpose

The transition into AI-era economics may produce social instability before it produces broad prosperity.

Key risks:

- middle-class collapse
- automation anxiety
- social fragmentation
- trust erosion
- post-labor instability

## Automation Shock Velocity

```text
ASV = WorkersDisplaced / TimeWindow
```

Fast displacement can produce disproportionately larger instability than slower displacement.

## AI Social Stability Index

Potential indicators:

- youth unemployment
- middle-class erosion
- AI anxiety
- wage stagnation
- compute concentration
- housing affordability

ASSI is intended as an AI-era civilization stability indicator.
