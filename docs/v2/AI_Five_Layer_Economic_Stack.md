# AI Five-Layer Economic Stack

## Layer 1: Energy Infrastructure

- power grids
- cooling systems
- nuclear / renewable energy
- data center land and water systems

## Layer 2: Compute & Semiconductor Layer

- GPUs
- TPUs
- inference clusters
- chip sovereignty
- compute providers

## Layer 3: Foundation Model Layer

- frontier models
- national models
- open-source models
- AI alignment systems

## Layer 4: Distribution & Platform Layer

- AI operating systems
- search layers
- AI browsers
- agent ecosystems
- cloud platforms

## Layer 5: Application & Agent Layer

- AI SaaS
- agents
- enterprise automation
- local productivity systems

## Policy Meaning

Each layer has different value extraction, monopoly risk, energy dependence, and social impact. Taxation should not treat all AI companies as the same kind of economic actor.
