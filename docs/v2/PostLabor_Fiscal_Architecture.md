# Post-Labor Fiscal Architecture

## Purpose

Post-labor fiscal architecture studies how tax systems must adapt when value creation shifts from human labor toward compute, automation, infrastructure, and capital ownership.

## Mismatch

The existing fiscal base is labor-centric:

- payroll tax
- income tax
- wage-linked social insurance
- employment-based benefits

AI may create a future where:

- GDP rises
- labor share falls
- wage growth stagnates
- taxable human labor shrinks
- automation profits concentrate

## New Fiscal Pillars

1. Compute Tax
2. Automation Tax
3. Sovereign AI Equity
4. AI Infrastructure Royalties
5. Public AI Dividend Systems

## Core Question

If machines, models, and compute infrastructure generate a growing share of economic value, how should the public recover enough value to preserve social stability?
