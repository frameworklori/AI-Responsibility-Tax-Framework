# Compute Feudalism Risk

## Thesis

Future inequality may emerge from control over compute infrastructure rather than direct ownership of labor.

Compute feudalism describes a scenario where:

- a small number of actors control compute infrastructure
- most firms and citizens rent access
- public institutions lack sovereign compute
- AI productivity gains concentrate upstream
- democratic fiscal systems lag behind value extraction

## Warning Signals

- rising Compute Concentration Index
- high dependence on foreign inference infrastructure
- platform lock-in
- compute rationing
- public sector inability to run national AI services
- widening gap between compute owners and compute renters

## Policy Response

- sovereign compute reserve
- public AI infrastructure
- AI infrastructure royalties
- public AI dividend systems
- antitrust scrutiny across the AI stack
