# Compute Feudalism

Compute feudalism describes a future where most people and firms do not own AI productivity infrastructure. They rent access from a small number of dominant infrastructure owners.

## Mechanism

- compute access concentrates
- model access is mediated through private APIs
- distribution runs through dominant platforms
- local inference capacity remains weak
- public institutions lack sovereign compute

## Risk

Productivity rises, but ownership of productivity infrastructure concentrates. This creates a rent-access economy where downstream firms and workers pay tolls to participate in AI production.

## Countermeasures

- public compute reserves
- sovereign AI cloud
- open-source model support
- local inference capability
- interoperability mandates
- public procurement for open systems
