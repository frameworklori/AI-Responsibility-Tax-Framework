# Public Thought Debate Status

AIRTF is a public thought debate and transition diagnostic framework.

It should remain revisable, evidence-based, and explicit about uncertainty. Current signals are mixed. Some patterns are already observable, some are medium-term risks, and some remain contingent scenarios.

AIRTF does not treat any pathway as inevitable. It maps current signals, structural mechanisms, possible futures, indicators to watch, and falsification conditions.

## Already Observable

- white-collar compression signals
- asset-market vs household liquidity divergence
- deficit support as liquidity buffer

## Medium-Term Risks

- permanent renter class
- demand insufficiency hidden by AI cost reduction and government support
- entry-level career scarring

## Contingent Scenarios

- civilization liquidity collapse
- access infrastructure feudalism
- post-wage subsidized stagnation

## Diagnostic Discipline

AIRTF should distinguish:

- observable signals from forecasts
- transition risks from proven outcomes
- policy hypotheses from settled prescriptions
- philosophical reflection from measurable economic mapping

Tax architecture, compute levies, sovereign AI equity, labor transition infrastructure, and access governance should remain active hypotheses that can be strengthened, revised, or falsified by evidence.
