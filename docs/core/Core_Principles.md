# Core Principles

1. AI acceleration requires accelerated responsibility.
2. Correlation between AI growth and GDP does not guarantee social stability.
3. Compute is becoming a strategic public resource.
4. AI value extraction is layered, not flat.
5. Public AI infrastructure preserves public optionality.
6. Open-source AI reduces rent-access dependency.
7. Labor transition requires income, roles, identity, and meaning.
8. Demand sustainability matters when labor income weakens.
9. Small nations need realistic implementation paths.
10. Multilateral coordination is needed for capital-mobile AI infrastructure.
