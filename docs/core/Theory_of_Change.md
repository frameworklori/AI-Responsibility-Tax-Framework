# Theory of Change

## Path from Theory to Policy

AIRTF can move from theory to policy in stages:

1. Define AI-era value extraction layers.
2. Measure compute concentration and labor displacement velocity.
3. Build public AI infrastructure before dependency becomes irreversible.
4. Introduce narrow levies where attribution is strongest.
5. Use revenue for transition infrastructure and public AI capacity.
6. Coordinate internationally as compute and capital become mobile.

## Which Countries Can Implement First?

Small and mid-sized countries can begin with transparency, compute dependency mapping, public AI infrastructure, and narrow public procurement rules.

Larger economies can implement compute concentration levies, antitrust scrutiny, and sovereign AI equity tools earlier because they have more market leverage.

## Policies Realistic for Small Nations

- national AI cloud pilots
- public inference infrastructure
- open-source AI procurement
- compute dependency audits
- automation shock monitoring
- sector-specific transition funds

## Policies Requiring Coordination

G20, OECD, IMF, and regional coordination may be needed for:

- cross-border compute taxation
- capital flight prevention
- compute relocation avoidance
- common reporting standards
- acquisition velocity disclosure

## Risks

- capital flight
- regulatory capture
- relocation of compute infrastructure
- public sector technical weakness
- private dependency becoming irreversible

## Strategic Priority

Create public alternatives before private dependency becomes irreversible.
