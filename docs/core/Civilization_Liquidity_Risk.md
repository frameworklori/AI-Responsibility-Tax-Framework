# Civilization Liquidity Risk

Civilization Liquidity Risk describes a condition where an economy remains productive on paper but loses broad circulation momentum.

## Paradox

The AI era may produce a paradox:

- productivity rises
- GDP rises
- stock markets rise
- AI capability rises

while:

- broad purchasing power weakens
- middle-class consumption slows
- credit dependence increases
- demand becomes fragile
- wealth and infrastructure ownership concentrate

## Why It Matters

The mass-consumption base depends on many households spending income back into the economy. If AI gains accrue mainly to infrastructure owners, the economy can become richer in aggregate but weaker in circulation.

## Current-Signal Layer

AIRTF should not only be read as an abstract AI-tax framework. It can also function as a live diagnostic system for economies where:

- AI productivity rises faster than wage circulation
- asset markets remain strong while household liquidity weakens
- energy and military sectors profit from geopolitical shocks
- government deficits substitute for weakened private purchasing power
- imports remain high despite domestic labor compression
- stock-market resilience hides demand fragility
