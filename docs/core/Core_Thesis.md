# Core Thesis

AI does not automatically destroy the economy. The deeper risk is that productivity gains may concentrate faster than purchasing power can circulate.

Modern consumer economies survive not only because goods are produced. They survive because money keeps circulating among broad consumer groups.

## Central Mechanism

```text
AI productivity rises
-> AI infrastructure ownership concentrates
-> capital owners capture productivity gains
-> white-collar labor income compresses
-> middle-class purchasing power weakens
-> money velocity slows
-> demand fragility increases
-> social and political instability rises
```

This mechanism is the central AIRTF transition risk.

## Civilization Liquidity Risk

Civilization Liquidity Risk occurs when productivity, GDP, asset values, and AI capability rise while broad purchasing power and money circulation weaken.

It is not only an inequality problem. It is a circulation problem.
