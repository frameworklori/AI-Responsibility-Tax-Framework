# AI-Era Scarcity Model

AI compresses execution, synthesis, coding, and recombination. Human value may increasingly shift toward strategic scarcity.

## Human Strategic Value in the AI Era

Human value may increasingly come from:

- defining new problems
- creating new abstraction layers
- trust-weighted judgment
- accountability
- institutional responsibility
- cross-domain synthesis
- embodied creation
- social legitimacy
- moral and legal liability

AI can produce outputs at scale, but societies still need humans to define what should be built, who is accountable, and which goals deserve legitimacy.
