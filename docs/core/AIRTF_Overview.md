# AIRTF Overview

AIRTF is now an AI-era economic sovereignty and infrastructure governance framework.

It studies how societies can regulate, redistribute, and stabilize value in an AI-integrated economy.

The repo keeps the original AI Responsibility Tax concept, but expands beyond a single tax proposal into a broader architecture covering compute sovereignty, platform power, public AI infrastructure, labor transition, and demand sustainability.

## Primary Question

How can societies capture enough AI-era value to preserve democratic capacity, public infrastructure, and social cohesion while AI productivity accelerates?

## Main Risk

The main risk is not only that AI replaces tasks. The deeper risk is that AI productivity infrastructure becomes concentrated upstream while most people and firms rent access downstream.
