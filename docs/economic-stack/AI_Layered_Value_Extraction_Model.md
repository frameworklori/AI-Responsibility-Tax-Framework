# AI Layered Value Extraction Model

The AI Layered Value Extraction Model evaluates each layer by:

- profit density
- monopoly risk
- social impact
- labor displacement potential
- infrastructure dependency
- public-interest sensitivity

| Layer | Profit Density | Monopoly Risk | Social Impact | Tax Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | High | High | National | Medium |
| Compute | Extreme | Extreme | Global | Very High |
| Models | High | Medium | Global | Medium |
| Platforms | Extreme | Extreme | Civilization-scale | Very High |
| Applications | Variable | Low-Medium | Local/Industry | Case-based |

Tax priority should rise when a layer combines high profit density, high infrastructure dependency, and high public-interest sensitivity.
