# Simulations — AIRTF Model Scenarios  
### AI-Responsibility-Tax Framework (AIRTF)  
### Version: 1.0

This folder contains **policy simulation datasets** and **Python scripts** for evaluating the global fiscal impacts of the AI-Responsibility-Tax Framework (AIRTF).  
The simulations model how different regions experience AI-driven economic changes through three primary variables:

- **P — Profit from AI industries**
- **U — Unemployment Impact caused by automation**
- **E — Energy Consumption from compute and model training**

These inputs allow AIRTF to compute Responsibility Tax (RT), redistribution outcomes, and cross-regional comparisons.

# 🌍 Purpose of the Simulation Scenarios

The files in `country-scenarios/` are **not real financial data** but are **policy-model estimates** derived from:

- The structure of regional AI industries  
- Compute adoption speed  
- Labor market vulnerability  
- Energy/grid constraints  
- Frontier model development capability  

This makes them ideal for *whitepapers, policy modeling, academic demonstration, and sensitivity analysis*.

---

# 🧩 AIRTF Core Formula Used in All Simulations

\[
RT = \alpha P + \beta U + \gamma E
\]

Where:

| Variable | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **P** | Annual profit generated by AI industries or AI-integrated sectors |
| **U** | Economic cost of job displacement → retraining, transition funding, wage gaps |
| **E** | Energy consumption or compute-driven carbon cost |
| **α, β, γ** | Policy coefficients — adjustable per country |

---

# 🔎 How Scenario Numbers Were Estimated

Each region uses a **different structural logic** reflecting its economic and technological landscape.

---

## 🇺🇸 **United States — High-AI, High-Compute Model**

The US scenarios assume:

- Large AI corporate profits (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, xAI)
- High unemployment impact due to rapid automation
- Very high energy consumption from frontier model training
- Larger β (labor impact) and γ (compute carbon cost)

📌 Result: **High P, High U, High E**

---

## 🇪🇺 **European Union — Sustainability-Weighted Model**

The EU scenarios assume:

- Moderate AI corporate profit  
- Slower automation and stronger labor protections  
- High sensitivity to carbon footprint → larger γ  
- Emphasis on green-compute transitions  

📌 Result: **Moderate P, Lower U, High γ**

---

## 🌏 **Asia — Mixed-Economy Model**

Asia includes high-tech nations (Japan, Korea, Singapore) and emerging economies (SEA manufacturing), so the numbers reflect:

- Mid-to-high AI adoption  
- High manufacturing automation impact → U elevation  
- Moderate compute energy  

📌 Result: **Mixed P, Medium-High U, Medium E**
